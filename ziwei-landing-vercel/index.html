07 / QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?+

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a traditional Chinese system of life interpretation based on birth information. It is used to explore different areas of life, personal tendencies and timing cycles.

Do I need to know my exact birth time?+

An accurate birth time can make a traditional chart more precise. If you do not know it, you can still ask a question and we can explain what information is useful for the type of exploration you are seeking.

Is this fortune telling?+

It is rooted in a traditional Chinese divination and metaphysical system, but the approach here is centered on reflection, patterns and timing rather than presenting the future as fixed or certain.

How many free questions are available each day?+

Only 10 free personal questions are opened each day. The daily availability may close once all places have been taken.

What can I ask about?+

You can ask about relationships, career, personal direction, recurring patterns, periods of change, or another personal topic that you would like to reflect on.

Are readings guaranteed predictions?+

No. A reading is not a guaranteed prediction of future events. It is an interpretive practice intended to offer perspective and a different way of looking at personal patterns and timing.

Readings are intended for personal reflection, cultural exploration and spiritual purposes. They should not replace professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.
