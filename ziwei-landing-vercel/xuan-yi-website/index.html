12 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Questions before
the first question.

Is this a religious practice?+

No. The website presents Chinese philosophical and metaphysical traditions as cultural systems for interpretation and reflection. It does not require religious belief or religious participation.

Are you saying the future is fixed?+

No. A chart or divination tradition is an interpretive framework. We do not present outcomes as guaranteed or unavoidable.

Why use Daoist ideas?+

Daoist thought provides an important historical vocabulary for ideas such as Yin-Yang, change, balance and the relationship between human life and the wider world.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?+

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a traditional Chinese birth-chart system that organizes symbolic stars and life domains into a structured chart used for interpretation and timing.

Can I explore without knowing the terminology?+

Yes. Begin with a personal question. The terminology is there to describe the framework, not to create a barrier.
