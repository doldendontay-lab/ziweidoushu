— ABOUT ZHEN LONG —

A Bridge Between
Ancient Wisdom and Modern Life

Zhen Long is a spiritual practitioner rooted in both Taoist and Buddhist traditions. Known for his deep understanding of traditional wisdom, he brings a distinctive and contemporary approach to navigating life — from resolving personal obstacles and restoring harmony in the home to exploring destiny, relationships, and the path ahead.

As one of a new generation of young practitioners, he bridges ancient wisdom with a modern perspective, helping people understand themselves more deeply and create meaningful changes in their lives.

We believe that ancient wisdom still has the power to guide us today — helping you understand yourself, your relationships, your career, and the timing of your life journey.

04 · THE PRACTITIONER

A human presence
behind the work.

In the East, there is a master known as Zhenlong, whose spiritual journey has taken him across Southeast Asia.

With years of dedicated practice, he has studied more than a dozen traditional spiritual practices, including Tianyan Tong — the ability to perceive beyond ordinary sight — and Tā Xīn Shù, the practice of perceiving the thoughts and intentions of others.

He is also said to have received teachings from Ji Gong and the Great Sage, Sun Wukong. His path draws from Theravada Buddhism, Mahayana compassion, and the traditions of Taoist cultivation, health, and spiritual practice.
