01 · Xuan Yi

A community from the East.

Xuan Yi is an Eastern spiritual community rooted in Buddhist and Daoist traditions, traditional wisdom, and systems of divination.

The purpose is simple: to give people a place to ask personal questions, explore their situation, and receive a perspective shaped by Eastern practice.

Long Shifu

At the heart of Xuan Yi is Long Shifu, a spiritual practitioner devoted to his path of practice. Within his tradition, he is associated with Tianyan Tong — the “heavenly eye,” a form of spiritual perception.

He is presented as a real practitioner rather than a virtual character. When available, people can speak with him directly through private conversation or video.
